A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clarity from
the beginning.

Can I get help with just one essay?+

Yes. Support can focus on a single piece of writing or on how your application materials work together. The starting point is what you need help expressing.

Do I need a finished draft to start?+

You can begin with ideas, notes, or an existing draft. We can help you reflect on your experiences, develop an outline, or revise writing you have already started.

What does essay coaching involve?+

Questions, structural feedback, and language suggestions to help you express your ideas clearly. You remain the author, and every claim should reflect your own experience. Admissions decisions rest with each institution.
